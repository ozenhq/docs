## What ozen does for you

<CardGrid stagger>
	<Card title="Writes everything down" icon="document">
		Every word of the call and of the people in the room, in Hebrew or English, a few seconds after it's said.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Knows who's talking" icon="comment">
		Recognizes voices. Tell it once who someone is and it remembers them in every future meeting.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Gets smarter as you go" icon="rocket">
		Fix a misheard word and ozen learns it. Fix the same mistake twice and it corrects it by itself.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Ask AI mid-meeting" icon="star">
		One click opens an AI assistant that has read the meeting so far and can see your screen.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Records only when you want" icon="setting">
		Always, only during calls, or by place: on at the office, off at home.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Private by design" icon="approve-check">
		Transcription happens on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded, and no one gets an "ozen joined the meeting" notice.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
