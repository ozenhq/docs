title: Your first meeting
description: From pressing Start to asking AI about the call.
---

import { Steps, Aside } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

<Steps>

1. **Press Start.** Click the ear in the menu bar, then **Start**. The ear turns red while ozen is recording.

2. **Join your call** in Zoom, Google Meet (in Chrome), Teams, Slack, FaceTime or Discord, as usual. There's
   nothing to connect: ozen hears those apps directly, and no one sees a bot join.

3. **Watch the transcript.** Click the ear to open the panel. Lines appear about 15–30 seconds after they're said,
   labeled `(call)` for people on the call and `(room)` for people near your Mac. Hebrew reads right to left.

4. **Name the speakers.** New voices show up as **S1**, **S2** and so on. Click a name and choose who it really
   is. ozen relabels everything that person said, and recognizes them in future meetings.
   [More on speakers](/guides/speakers/).

5. **Fix a word** if ozen misheard it: click the text and type the right words. ozen learns from it.
   [More on fixes](/guides/corrections/).

6. **Ask AI.** Press **Ask AI** to open an AI assistant that has read the meeting so far. Ask it things like
   *"What did we decide about the launch date?"* or *"What's on my screen right now?"*
   [More on AI](/guides/ai/).

7. **Press Stop** when you're done. The ear shows an hourglass while ozen finishes writing down the last few
   seconds, then it stops.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Tired of pressing Start?">
Switch the panel to **Meetings** and ozen starts by itself whenever a call begins, and stops when it ends.
See [When ozen records](/guides/recording/).
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution" title="Tell people you're recording">
ozen records other people. Let participants know, and follow your local recording laws.
See [Privacy](/help/privacy/).
</Aside>
