ozen is free. Download it from the download page, which also has the few Terminal commands that set it up.
You only do this once; plan on about 10 minutes, most of it waiting for downloads.

<LinkCard title="Download ozen" description="Pick your computer, then follow the install steps there." href="https://ozenhq.com/download/" />

## Allow ozen to listen

The first time you press **Start**, macOS asks for two permissions. Allow both, then press **Start** again.

| macOS asks for | Why ozen needs it |
|---|---|
| **Screen & System Audio Recording** | To hear the audio of your meeting apps. ozen never records video of your screen. |
| **Microphone** | To hear people in the room, including you. |

If you missed the pop-up, open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, find each of those, and turn on **Ozen**.

Later, if you set up [places](/guides/recording/#places), macOS also asks for **Location Services**.

<Aside title="The first meeting is slower">
ozen downloads its speech models (about 7 GB) the first time it transcribes. Until that finishes, lines appear
late and the panel may say it's catching up. After that, everything runs offline.
</Aside>

## Updating

When there's a new version, follow [Updating on the download page](https://ozenhq.com/download/#update).
Updating never interrupts a recording in progress, and your permissions stay.
