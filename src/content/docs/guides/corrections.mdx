## Fixing a line

Click the text of any line in the panel, type the right words, and press <kbd>Return</kbd>. To undo, clear the
text and press <kbd>Return</kbd>; the original comes back.

## ozen learns from your fixes

- **New words.** Names, product terms and jargon you type in a fix are remembered, and ozen listens for them in
  every new line.
- **Repeated mistakes.** Fix the same thing the same way twice, like "פי אר" to "PR", and ozen fixes it by itself
  from then on.
- **Undo a bad habit.** If ozen starts "fixing" something that was right, correct it back. It notices and stops.

## Teaching words ahead of time

Names and terms ozen should spell right live in a plain list: **`vocab.txt`** in your ozen folder. Open it with:

```sh
open -e ~/ozen/vocab.txt
```

Add words separated by commas or one per line, and save. ozen reads it continuously. The names of people you've
tagged are included automatically.

<Aside type="tip">
English words said in the middle of Hebrew are the hardest for ozen. If you use a term often, add it to the list.
</Aside>

## Hebrew and English

ozen decides for every sentence whether it's Hebrew or English, and uses a speech model trained for that language.
Mixed sentences work, with the English-in-Hebrew caveat above.
