ozen can hand your meetings to an AI assistant, **Claude Code** or **Hermes**, which reads the transcript and
answers your questions. You need one of them installed first.

## Ask about the meeting happening now

Press **Ask AI** in the panel. A Terminal window opens with the assistant, already holding the meeting so far.
It keeps up as the meeting continues and can look at your screen. Try:

- *"Summarize the last five minutes."*
- *"What did Dana ask me to do?"*
- *"What's on my screen, and does it match what they're describing?"*
- *"Draft a follow-up email with the decisions we made."*

## Past meetings

<Steps>
1. Switch the panel to **Meetings**.
2. Select one or several (hold <kbd>⌘</kbd> or <kbd>⇧</kbd> while clicking).
3. Press **Open** and pick the assistant.
</Steps>

Great for *"What did we promise the client across these three calls?"* or *"Write the weekly update."*

Each opened set is saved as a folder; double-click its **claude.command** or **hermes.command** file to come
back to it later.

**Auto add with Kev** finds other meetings about the same project and adds them for you. It needs the Kev helper
running on your Mac; without it, this option does nothing.

<Aside type="caution" title="What the AI sees">
A cloud assistant like Claude sends what it reads to its provider. Only the meetings you choose are shared.
See [Privacy](/help/privacy/).
</Aside>

## Other AI apps

Any AI app that supports MCP (a common way for AI apps to use tools) can read and edit ozen's meetings, people and
settings. Setup is in [MCP server](/developers/mcp/).
