ozen lives in the menu bar as an ear. **Click** it to open the panel. **Right-click** it for a quick menu with
the same controls.

<PanelDemo />

## What the ear means

<IconStates />

When ozen isn't recording, the panel says **Not recording** and why.

## Buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Start** | Start listening to the call and the room. |
| **Pause** / **Resume** | Take a break. Resuming is instant. |
| **Stop** | Stop listening. ozen finishes writing down what it already heard first. |
| **Always / Meetings** | Choose when ozen records. See [When ozen records](/guides/recording/). |
| **Places…** | Record differently at home, at work, anywhere. See [Places](/guides/recording/#places). |
| **Review** | Answer who said the lines ozen is unsure about. See [Review](/guides/speakers/#review). |
| **Ask AI** | Ask an AI assistant about the meeting happening now. See [AI assistants](/guides/ai/). |

## Three views

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Transcript">
The live transcript, updated every 2 seconds.

- Click a **name** to say who really said that line, or to ignore that voice.
- Click the **text** to fix it.
- An orange **?** means ozen isn't sure who spoke.
- Grey lines are voices you chose to ignore.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Timeline">
One row per person with their total talk time, and a bar for everything they said. When people talk over each
other, their bars overlap.

- **−** and **+** zoom in and out.
- Long silences (over 2 minutes) shrink to a small gap.
- Hover a bar to read it; click it to jump to that line.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Meetings">
All your past meetings, newest first. A silence of 10 minutes or more starts a new meeting.

Select one or several (hold <kbd>⌘</kbd> or <kbd>⇧</kbd> while clicking) and press **Open** to hand them to an
AI assistant. See [AI assistants](/guides/ai/#past-meetings).

The bottom of the panel shows how accurately ozen recognizes voices, now and when you started.
</TabItem>
</Tabs>

<Aside title="Warnings">
If something's wrong, like a missing permission or a silent microphone, a warning appears at the top of the panel.
[Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting/) explains each one.
</Aside>
