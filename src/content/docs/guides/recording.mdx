## Two modes

Pick one with the **Always / Meetings** switch in the panel.

- **Always** records from the moment you press **Start** until you pause or stop, including everything said near
  your Mac.
- **Meetings** starts by itself when Zoom, Chrome (Google Meet), Teams, Slack, FaceTime or Discord starts using your
  microphone, and stops 20 seconds after the call ends. No calendar or plugin needed.

<ModeDemo />

Pressing Pause or Start yourself always wins, until the next meeting starts or ends.

## What ozen hears

| | What it is | Written down? |
|---|---|---|
| **Call** | Sound from your meeting apps | Yes, marked `(call)` |
| **Room** | Your microphone: you and anyone near your Mac | Yes, marked `(room)` |
| **Computer** | Every other app, like a video or music | No. ozen only uses it to avoid mistaking your speakers for a person in the room |

## Places

Places let ozen behave differently depending on where you are. Open **Places…** from the panel.

Each place has a name, a spot on the map, a size, and one of three settings:

| Setting | While you're there |
|---|---|
| **Auto record** | Always record |
| **Record meetings only** | Record only during calls |
| **Auto off** | Never record |

Inside a place, its setting replaces Always / Meetings. Outside all places, your usual mode applies.

**Home** and **Work** are already in the list, waiting for you to set where they are. To place one, either:

- press **Use current location** while you're there,
- press **Pick on map** and click the spot,
- drag its pin on the map, or
- type its latitude and longitude.

<Aside type="note">
The first time, macOS asks to let Ozen use your location. Your location stays on your Mac. Showing the map
downloads map images of that area from OpenStreetMap.
</Aside>
