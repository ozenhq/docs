---
title: Known limits
description: What ozen can't do (yet).
---

- **A short delay.** Lines appear about 15–30 seconds after they're said.
- **English inside Hebrew** is the hardest case. Add common terms to your [word list](/guides/corrections/#teaching-words-ahead-of-time).
  Voices far from the Mac are hard to hear.
- **Talking over the computer.** If you talk over someone on the call and your voices sound alike, your words may
  be dropped as echo.
- **Speak Selection.** When macOS reads text aloud with Speak Selection, ozen hears it as a person in the room.
  Use **Ignore this voice**.
- **Two at a time.** ozen separates two people talking at once; a third joins one of them. Very short overlaps
  (under 2 seconds) aren't separated.
- **Mac only**, with an Apple chip and macOS 15 or later.
