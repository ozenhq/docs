ozen is built so your meetings stay yours. Speech is transcribed and voices are recognized on your Mac. No bot
joins your call, and no audio is uploaded.

## Recording other people

<Aside type="caution" title="Your responsibility">
ozen records and transcribes other people. **Tell participants you're recording, and follow your local laws.**
In some places everyone must agree to be recorded.
</Aside>

- In **Always** mode, ozen hears everything near your Mac, not only meetings. Use **Meetings** mode or
  [places](/guides/recording/#places) to limit it.
- Voiceprints are sensitive personal data, like a fingerprint. Only name people who are fine with it, and keep
  your voices repository private.

## Removing data

- **One meeting or some lines:** ask an [AI assistant](/guides/ai/) connected to ozen to delete them, or use the
  [MCP tools](/developers/mcp/).
- **A person:** clear their name everywhere with the `delete_person` [MCP tool](/developers/mcp/), and remove
  them from your voices repository so your other Macs forget them too.
- **Everything:** quit ozen and delete the `ozen` folder in your home folder.
