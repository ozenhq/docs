When something's wrong, a warning appears at the top of the panel. Find it below.

## “Recording blocked”

ozen doesn't have permission to hear your meeting apps.

1. Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording**.
2. Turn on **Ozen**.
3. Press **Start** again.

If people in the room aren't showing up, do the same under **Microphone**.

## “Recording on hold: the screen is asleep or locked”

macOS pauses ozen's listening while your screen sleeps or is locked. It picks up again when you wake your Mac.

## “Microphone is silent”

The microphone your Mac is using isn't picking up sound. Open **System Settings › Sound › Input** and choose
another one.

If the warning says ozen is **using** one microphone **because** another is silent, ozen already switched to one
that works. You only need to act if you prefer a different one.

## “Transcriber stopped” or “catching up”

- **Stopped:** the part of ozen that writes things down crashed. It restarts by itself and nothing you said is
  lost.
- **Catching up:** it's running behind, which is normal in your first meeting while speech models download.
  Lines arrive late, but they all arrive.

## Nothing from the call shows up

- Is the call in Zoom, Chrome, Teams, Slack, FaceTime or Discord? Calls in other apps, like Safari, aren't written
  down.
- In **Meetings** mode, ozen waits until the meeting app turns on your microphone. Press **Start** to record right
  away.

## Wrong names

Name a couple of lines per person and answer **Review**. Each answer relabels the whole meeting.
See [Who is speaking](/guides/speakers/).

## A video, or my Mac reading aloud, shows up as a person

Click its name and choose **Ignore this voice**.

## Permissions disappeared after an update

Update the way [Updating](/start/install/#updating) describes, which keeps permissions. Copying the app by hand
doesn't.
